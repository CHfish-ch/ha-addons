What sun elevation does your awning actually need?

As the sun drops, an awning's shadow line climbs the wall and it covers less and less of the window — from the bottom up. Set your own measurements below to see where yours stands. On most awnings the fabric hangs below the window head, so it shades something at every sun height and never needs gating; that is why the add-on has no option for this. A flat awning or fixed canopy sits high enough that it genuinely stops helping, and the first figure below is the condition to put in your automation.
